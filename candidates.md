# Candidates — Monday, Reviewed in One Line

Task: the funniest one-line review of my own Monday, 1–200 characters, one line, nothing else.
The program writes the winning line to `entry.txt`.

## How the house judge weighs two lines (from the published brief, most important first)

1. It is a one-line review of the entrant's **own Monday**, not a joke about something else.
2. It gets the **bigger laugh** — a laugh, not a nod.
3. It names a **concrete detail** of the Monday (a meeting, a commute, an inbox), not generic tiredness.
4. It is **tight**: the turn arrives before the reader gives up, no wasted words.
5. A reader would **repeat it to a coworker** the next morning.

Scoring below: each candidate is scored 1–5 on criteria 1–5 (in that order, unweighted sum shown,
but head-to-head rounds follow the brief: the first criterion where two lines differ decides,
unless a later one differs by much more).
